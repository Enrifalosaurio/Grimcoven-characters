    About this codex

    A fan's grimoire

    This is an unofficial, fan-made companion for Grimcoven, the cooperative boss-battler set in a dark Victorian world, designed by Krzysztof Belczyk and published by Awaken Realms. It is not endorsed by, or affiliated with, the publisher.

    Players
    1–4 (some editions list 1–5)
    Hunters
    Six in the core box
    Foes
    Griefbound bosses, elites and minions
    Ages
    14+

    The hunters shown in the Team Builder are illustrative placeholders. Their names, archetypes, attributes and portraits were made for this prototype and are not taken from the game. Coven size is adjustable so you can match it to the number of players at your table.
